# CAD Agent — Hardware Design & DfM Screening

Provides mechanical calculations, DfM screening, and validation tools for CAD modeling:
- **Overhang screening**: Verify faces against 45° overhang limits for additive manufacturing.
- **Hole clearance checks**: Verify nominal hole diameters and clearance tolerances for fasteners and dowels.
- **Snap-fit calculator**: Cantilever root strain and deflection calculations to prevent failure.
- **Heat-set insert sizing**: Hole depth, diameter, and pull-out strength sizing for brass inserts (Ruthex, CNC Kitchen).
- **Rolling bearing checks**: Bearing fit tolerances and L10 rating life calculations.
